Announcing Chud AI 2.0: now with native palate detection

MS
Madison Slop
VP of Chud AI

Today we're shipping Chud AI 2.0, a ground-up rewrite of our chud-native LLM. v2 is faster, mumblier, and dramatically less coherent than v1. It is, by every internal benchmark, the worst-communicating model on the market. We are extremely proud of it.

What's new in 2.0

Native palate detection

Chud AI 2.0 ships with a built-in palate-position classifier. Before generating a response, the model assesses the operator's tongue position via WebRTC microphone leak. If the tongue is detected on the palate (i.e. the operator is actively mewing), the model responds with extra mumbles to match the user's energy. If the tongue is on the floor, the model assumes the operator is "fully chudded" and shortens all responses to fewer than 12 tokens.

Sub-mumble token streaming

v1 streamed completed words. v2 streams individual mumbles. This gives the user the perceptual experience of a real chud trying to form a sentence but giving up halfway through, which is a 47% improvement in our internal "is this a chud?" benchmark.

$ chudflare mewing-ai --stream "what's the weather like"
m...
mm...
mmm...
i'd check but
i'd check but it's
i'd check but it's fi
i'd check but it's f

Agartha-mode

v2 includes a configurable agartha_mode parameter. When set to true, the model responds to every query with exactly the word "agartha". This is useful for users who find v1's responses too informative.

Improved hunch awareness

v2 dynamically adjusts response coherence based on the time of day. Responses at 9am are slightly more articulate (still incoherent, but you can almost parse them). Responses after 8pm degrade into pure mumble. This matches the natural hunch curve of a working chud.
