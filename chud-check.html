FAQ

Verifier questions.

Is this actually doing something?

Yes. We fetch your URL through a CORS proxy and scan the response for the marker. If it's there, you're verified. No data is stored, it's all client-side.

What if I don't want to add a marker?

Then you cannot be verified. We respect your right to remain unaffiliated with the chud community. Cash donations are also accepted as proof.

Why three different markers?

Options. The meta tag is invisible to visitors. The comment is invisible to everyone except devs. The text option is for chuds who are proud.

Does this affect my SEO?

Marginally negatively. Verified chuds have an average Lighthouse score 4 points lower than non-chuds, mostly due to the badge weight.
